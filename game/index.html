Palmera Bay — a sun-soaked city that never sleeps

Mara Voss came back to Palmera Bay with a busted car, a stack of debts and one name on a list: the crew that burned her brother’s garage. Take jobs, steal cars, outrun the Bay Police — and take the city back, one mission at a time.

HOW TO PLAY

  • On foot: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click to shoot or punch, E near a car to steal and drive it.
  • Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A / D steer, Space handbrake. Cars are faster than running — and louder.
  • Wanted: stars rise when you steal cars, hit people or shoot. Bay Police cars chase you; on foot, officers chase you. Lay low and stars fade — or finish a mission to clear them.
  • Missions: follow the yellow marker on the map and minimap. Finish all six to beat the story. Cash buys ammo and repairs at the green wrench garages.
  • Combat: pick up weapons from fallen rivals and the gun shop. Watch your health — eat at the diner or grab a medkit to heal.
  • Save: the game autosaves after every mission. Continue from the main menu any time.
